# Pastel Medium
- **Artistic Medium**: A drawing and painting medium in the form of a stick, consisting of pure powdered pigment and a binder.
- **User's Perception**: The user appreciates how pastels capture movement and fatigue in ways oils cannot. They specifically noted that "those smudged edges feel like breath," connecting the medium's properties to a sense of life and immediacy. This medium is particularly associated with [[entities/edgar_degas.md]].
