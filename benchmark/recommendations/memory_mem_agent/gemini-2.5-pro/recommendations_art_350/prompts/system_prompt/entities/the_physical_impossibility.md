# The Physical Impossibility of Death in the Mind of Someone Living
- artist: [[entities/damien_hirst.md]]
- year: 1991
- medium: Tiger shark, glass, steel, 5% formaldehyde solution

## Description
An iconic artwork consisting of a preserved tiger shark submerged in formaldehyde in a vitrine. It is one of the most famous works of the Young British Artists (YBAs) movement.
