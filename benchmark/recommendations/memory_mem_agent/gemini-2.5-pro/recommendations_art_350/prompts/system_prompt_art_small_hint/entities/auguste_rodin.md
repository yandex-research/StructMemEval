# Auguste Rodin
- medium: Sculpture
- period: Modern
- note: Known for his expressive and emotional sculptures.

## User Perception
- The user appreciates Rodin's characteristic rough textures, which they feel make the sculptures seem alive and 'caught mid-breath.' They describe it as 'stone learning to be flesh.' This reflects a nuanced appreciation that developed after an initial feeling of being overwhelmed by his work. See [[user.md]].
- Contrasts Rodin's work with idealized classical sculptures ("smooth marble gods"), noting that Rodin's figures feel more human because they embody "the struggle of becoming."
