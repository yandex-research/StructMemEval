# Rembrandt van Rijn
- era: Dutch Golden Age
## Key Works
- The Night Watch
- The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp
## Themes
- Portraiture, biblical scenes, self-portraits
## Self-Portraits
- Rembrandt created nearly 100 self-portraits over 40 years, documenting his own aging process from a young, successful artist to a troubled old man. They serve as a profound visual autobiography.
## User's Perspective
- Values Rembrandt's work for its beautiful and honest depiction of "the cost of being human," which stands in stark contrast to the curated perfection of modern celebrity culture.
