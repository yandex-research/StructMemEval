## Design Philosophy
- The adversary system creates tension without desperation when calibrated properly.
- Player agency throughout the entire game. No one should feel like they're just waiting for their turn to matter.


## Interaction Preferences
- Dislikes interactions that feel robotic or like just following instructions. Prefers a more natural and less rigid conversational style.

- Prefers collaborative problem-solving, where each round feels like solving a puzzle together, rather than one person dictating a plan.
