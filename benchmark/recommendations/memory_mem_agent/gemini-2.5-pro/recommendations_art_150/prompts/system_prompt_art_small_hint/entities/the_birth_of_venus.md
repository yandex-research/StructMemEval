# The Birth of Venus
- Artist: [[entities/sandro_botticelli.md]]
- Period: c. 1484–1486
- Medium: Tempera on canvas
- Location: Uffizi Gallery, Florence

## User Interpretation
- The user sees Venus's pose not as prudishness but as a representation of the "universal hesitation before becoming someone new."
- They feel the figure, despite her divine context ("floats on a shell"), is grounded in a deep sense of "human vulnerability."
