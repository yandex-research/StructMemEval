# The Starry Night
- artist: Vincent van Gogh
- year: 1889
- medium: Oil on canvas
- movement: Post-Impressionism

## User's Interpretation
- The user sees the painting as "emotional weather," where the swirling skies represent hope fighting through mental storms.
- They feel it makes beauty from turbulence.
- Contrasts the experience of seeing it in person versus a reproduction:
  - **Reproduction**: "a pretty night sky"
  - **In person**: "a document of human struggle transformed into beauty"
