# Edgar Degas
- **Artistic Style**: French Impressionist artist
- **Famous for**: Paintings, sculptures, prints, and drawings. He is especially identified with the subject of dance; more than half of his works depict dancers.
- **Medium**: Pastel

## User's Perspective
- [[user.md]] appreciates how Degas captures dancers in unguarded moments, showing their exhaustion and concentration.
- [[user.md]] values that Degas finds beauty in the labor behind perfection rather than just the polished final performance.
