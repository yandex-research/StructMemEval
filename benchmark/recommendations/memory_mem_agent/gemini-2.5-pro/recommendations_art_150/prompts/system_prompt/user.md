## Artistic Interpretations
- **Sandro Botticelli**: The user has strong opinions on Botticelli's work. They feel his restraint makes emotions more potent and appreciate the human vulnerability in his figures. They find themselves returning to his work when modern art feels too loud or aggressive. [[entities/botticelli.md]]
- **Monet vs. Renoir**: Monet shows nature's truth; Renoir shows nature's postcard. One invites contemplation, the other just pretty decoration.
- **Rembrandt**: Unlike curated celebrity images today, Rembrandt shows the cost of being human—and makes it beautiful. [[entities/rembrandt.md]]
  - His use of thick impasto is not for show, but to accurately capture how light falls on aged skin.
- **Mark Rothko**: The user agrees with Rothko's philosophy that silence can be the most powerful form of communication in art. [[entities/rothko.md]]
- **Rothko vs. Pollock**: Rothko makes the user feel something immediately, while [[entities/jackson_pollock.md]] makes them wonder what they are supposed to feel.
