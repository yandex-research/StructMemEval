# Sandro Botticelli
- Style: Early Renaissance painter
- Noted for: linear grace of his paintings

## User's Interpretations
- The user finds that Botticelli's restraint, in contrast to later Baroque drama, makes the emotion in his work more potent, drawing the viewer in rather than overwhelming them.
- The user believes Venus's modest gesture in "The Birth of Venus" signifies a universal hesitation before transformation, a vulnerability that feels grounded and human. [[entities/birth_of_venus.md]]
- The user describes his style as having a "linear grace" where the figures create a 'rhythm that guides your eye like poetry on a page.'
