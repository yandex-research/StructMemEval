# Henri Matisse
- **Nationality**: French
- **Known for**: Use of color, fluid drawing style, and paper cut-outs (gouaches découpées).

## Major Works
- **Cut-Outs**: A significant body of late work. The user believes they have healing properties. [[entities/matisse_cut_outs.md]]

## Reflections
- "The cut-outs feel like pure joy made visible—no pretension, just color and form dancing together."
