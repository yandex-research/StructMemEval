## Games
- Enjoys strategy games that require deep thinking and long-term planning.
- Prefers games with depth but not unnecessary complexity or rulebooks that take hours to learn.
- Would rather play a game multiple times to discover its depth than spend hours learning rules before the first play.
- When playing with serious gamers, the user will always choose a strategy game over a party game.
- Finds most party games too shallow and repetitive, enjoying them only occasionally and preferring games with more strategic satisfaction.
- Loves strategy games with variable setup or asymmetric factions that ensure each game feels fresh rather than repetitive (e.g., Root).
- Prefers strategy games with meaningful player interaction where decisions directly affect other players, rather than 'multiplayer solitaire'.

## Board Game Preferences
- **Core Principle: Distributed Agency.** Abhors cooperative games prone to "quarterbacking" (alpha players). Values games where all players have meaningful decisions and agency, regardless of genre (e.g., Spirit Island).
- **Complexity vs Depth:** Prefers strategic depth that emerges from simple rulesets. Wary of heavy, intimidating rulebooks, favoring games where strategies are discovered through play.
- **Replayability:** High preference for games with built-in variability (asymmetry, variable setup) that resist becoming "solved" or formulaic. Values an earned strategic arc over predictable openings.
