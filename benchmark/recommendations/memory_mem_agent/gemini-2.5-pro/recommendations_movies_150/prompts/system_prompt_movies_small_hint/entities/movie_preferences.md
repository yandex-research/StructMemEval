# Movie Preferences

## Liked Movies
- **Die Hard**: User considers it the "perfect Christmas movie". [[entities/die_hard.md]]
  - Rewatches it every December without fail.
  - Praises the villain payoff of Hans Gruber falling from the roof, calling it "perfect".
  - Thinks [[entities/alan_rickman.md]] was "incredible" in the role.
  - Believes every character in the movie has clear motives, and that the sequels never matched the original's quality.
- **Terminator 2: Judgment Day**: User believes it is the "gold standard for action films", highlighting that it shows [[entities/arnold_schwarzenegger.md]] at his "absolute peak". (Rewatched on 2024-07-25)
  - Thinks [[entities/robert_patrick.md]] was terrifying as the T-1000.
  - Believes the liquid metal effect is superior to modern CGI.
  - Considers [[entities/arnold_schwarzenegger.md]]'s presence and one-liners to be a key part of the movie's success.
- **Mad Max: Fury Road**: User noted it was filmed with real vehicles and practical effects, which aligns with their general preference. [[entities/fury_road.md]]
  - Specifically mentioned the "pole cats swinging between vehicles" scene as an example of incredible stunt work that required months of training.
- **Heat**: User considers it the "perfect action film." [[entities/heat.md]]
  - Praises the downtown shootout scene for using real blanks, which made the danger feel real.
  - Noted that you could "feel the weight of gunfire" and see "real bystander reactions."
  - Believes actors like De Niro and Pacino were genuinely reacting to the chaos, not just acting, which adds to the authenticity.
  - Contrasts this with modern CGI shootouts, which the user feels are "fake" in comparison.
  - Believes the film holds up after 25 years because it focused on character and practical filmmaking, not passing visual trends.
- **Mission: Impossible - Fallout**: Appreciates the realism of brimming with action fight scene with Henry Cavill. [[entities/mission_impossible_fallout.md]]
  - Noted that "you feel every punch because it was real." This aligns with their preference for practical, impactful action over CGI.
- **Mission: Impossible (Series)**: User states the series stands out because it uses real action over CGI. [[entities/mission_impossible_series.md]]

## General Preferences
- Strongly prefers practical effects and well-choreographed scenes (e.g., a truck chase) over "weightless CGI battles," believing the danger feels more real and impactful.
- Believes modern action stars lack the charisma of stars from a previous era.
- Prefers action heroes who feel human and vulnerable (e.g., John McClane), as their struggles are more believable than those of "invincible CGI" heroes.
- Believes practical effects age better than CGI, which is why older movies with practical effects still hold up well.
- Stated a clear preference for "Real stunts over CGI any day."
- Values strong character work and believes it matters as much as action. Dislikes that modern films often forget this.
- Appreciates when movies maintain tension even in slow scenes.
- Likes when stories ground fantastical or high-concept elements in tangible, emotional reality, which they describe as "practical effects with real stakes and character depth."
- Believes explosions should be felt physically ("shake your chest"), emphasizing the importance of powerful sound design over just visual spectacle.
- Prefers a single meaningful scene over an entire CGI battle, highlighting the importance of emotional weight and storytelling over pure spectacle.
