# Rembrandt van Rijn
- a Dutch Golden Age painter, printmaker, and draughtsman.
- genre: History painting, portraits, self-portraits

## Significance
- Known for his deeply personal and unflinching self-portraits that document his life from youth to old age, capturing his changing fortunes and psychological state.
- Master of chiaroscuro (the use of strong contrasts between light and dark).

## User Observations
- **Late Self-Portraits**: User appreciates that his late self-portraits depict aging without vanity, where "every wrinkle maps a life fully lived" and the light "forgives" rather than flatters.
