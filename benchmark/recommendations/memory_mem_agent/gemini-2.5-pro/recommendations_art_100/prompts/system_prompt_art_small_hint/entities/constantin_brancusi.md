# Constantin Brancusi
- nationality: Romanian
- movement: Modernism, Abstract Sculpture

## Notable Works

### Bird in Space
- **User's Interpretation**: The user believes this piece is not about the literal form of a bird (feathers, beak) but rather captures the pure essence of flight.
- **Material Significance**: The use of polished bronze is seen by the user as making the concept of "weightlessness tangible."
