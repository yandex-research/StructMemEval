# Board Game Preferences

## Design Philosophy & Preferences
- **Adversary Systems**: The adversary system should create tension without desperation when calibrated properly.
- Prefers when thematic elements remain meaningful and don't get reduced to purely mechanical tokens.
  - Example: "The disease cubes became tokens to move rather than threats to manage."
- Dislikes or is critical of games with low player interaction, which are sometimes described as "multiplayer solitaire".
  - Example Game: [[entities/terraforming_mars.md]]
- Dislikes games where opening moves are formulaic and lead to predictable outcomes.
  - Example: "The opening moves became too formulaic, and I could predict outcomes early." 
- **Economic Games**: Appreciates games where money represents meaningful trade-offs and spending decisions create genuine tension, rather than just serving as victory points.
- Appreciates mechanics that can soften the difficulty or "punishing" nature of a game.
  - Example: The tea mechanic in [[entities/brass_birmingham.md]] is preferred over the more punishing feel of [[entities/brass_lancashire.md]].
- Appreciates map variability for adding freshness and replayability.
- Values engaging cooperative mechanics; a strong theme alone is not enough to make a cooperative game successful if the cooperative experience itself is lacking.
- **Sustained Engagement**: Prefers games that maintain agency and engagement even during opponents' turns.
- **Resource Gathering**: Enjoys resource gathering when it feels like building an industrial empire.

## Specific Game Experiences
- **Just One**: Worked perfectly with mixed experience levels. [[entities/just_one.md]]
- **Robinson Crusoe**: The rulebook was intimidating to about half of the user's game group, suggesting a high complexity or difficult learning curve. [[entities/robinson_crusoe.md]]

## Memorable Quotes & Anecdotes
- A friend in the user's game group frequently says, "I'll take the action, you all feed the colony," every round in a game that likely has a "feeding" mechanic. This has become an in-joke.
## Disliked Mechanics
- **Quarterbacking / Alpha-Player Problem**: Dislikes cooperative games where simple mechanics and open information allow one player to solve the puzzle for the entire group, turning other players into bystanders.
  - Example Game: [[entities/forbidden_desert.md]]
