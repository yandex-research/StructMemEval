# Heat (1995 Film)
- genre: Action, Crime, Thriller
- director: [[entities/michael_mann.md]]
- key_facts:
  - The downtown shootout scene used real blanks, making it feel particularly dangerous and realistic.
  - User appreciates the impactful sound design of the shootout, noting the bass should be strong enough to "rattle the room."
  - The user believes no other film has surpassed the quality of this shootout scene.
  - The coffee shop scene is highly praised by the user for its display of character work between two master actors, [[entities/al_pacino.md]] and [[entities/robert_de_niro.md]].
