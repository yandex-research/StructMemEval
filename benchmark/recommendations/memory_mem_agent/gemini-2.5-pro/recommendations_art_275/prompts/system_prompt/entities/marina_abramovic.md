# Marina Abramović
- occupation: Performance Artist

## Themes
- **Endurance and Pain**: Her work often involves testing the limits of her physical and mental endurance.
- **Artist-Audience Relationship**: Explores the dynamic and energy between the performer and the audience.
- **The Gaze**: Unbroken eye contact is a recurring and powerful element in her performances.

## User Reflections
- "Her physical pain becomes spiritual power through that unbroken eye contact."
