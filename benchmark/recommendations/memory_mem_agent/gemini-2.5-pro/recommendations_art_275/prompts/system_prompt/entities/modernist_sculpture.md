# Modernist Sculpture
- **Description**: A style of sculpture that emerged in the late 19th century and peaked in the 20th century. It is characterized by a departure from traditional, realistic representations of the human form and a move towards abstraction, new materials, and an emphasis on form and space.

## Key Artists
- [[entities/auguste_rodin.md]]
- [[entities/constantin_brancusi.md]]
- Alberto Giacometti
- Henry Moore
- Barbara Hepworth

## Key Characteristics
- Abstraction
- Simplification of form
- Use of unconventional materials (e.g., steel, plastic)
- Emphasis on negative space
- Exploration of movement and dynamism
