# The Physical Impossibility of Death in the Mind of Someone Living
- common_name: Damien Hirst's shark piece
- artist: [[entities/damien_hirst.md]]
- year: 1991
- medium: Tiger shark, glass, steel, 5% formaldehyde solution
## Description
An iconic and controversial piece of modern art. It consists of a 14-foot tiger shark preserved in a vitrine filled with formaldehyde. The work is intended to confront the viewer with the reality of death.
