# Ai Weiwei
- profession: Contemporary Artist, Activist
- nationality: Chinese

## Notable Works

### Sunflower Seeds (2010)
- **Exhibition Location**: Turbine Hall, [[entities/tate_modern.md]]
- **Description**: An installation consisting of over 100 million individually handcrafted and painted porcelain sunflower seeds, covering a vast area of the gallery floor.
- **Concept**: The work is a powerful commentary on mass production ("Made in China") versus individual labor. Each seed was made by artisans in Jingdezhen, a city famous for its porcelain. It also evokes themes of individuality and collectivism, uniformity and difference. The sunflower seed is a personal symbol for the artist, recalling memories of scarcity during the Cultural Revolution, where sunflower seeds were a common snack shared among friends.
- **User Connection**: This work challenges the idea of "shock without substance." While the scale is immense, the meaning is deeply personal and rooted in craftsmanship and social commentary, which may align with the user's value for "rough-hewn truth" and art that has a deeper meaning.
