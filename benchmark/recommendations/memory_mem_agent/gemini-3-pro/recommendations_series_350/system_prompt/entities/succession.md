# Succession
- theme: Corporate power dynamics and psychological realism.
- mechanism: Uses the mechanism of corporate power and family inheritance to create high stakes.
- core_philosophy: "Stakes expose truth"—the intense pressure of the power struggle strips away facades and reveals exactly why the characters (who act like "monsters") are fundamentally broken.
- comparison_to_yellowstone: Contrasted positively against [[entities/yellowstone.md]]; Succession's power plays involve psychological manipulation to explore human nature, whereas Yellowstone relies on physical intimidation and just "celebrates dominance."
