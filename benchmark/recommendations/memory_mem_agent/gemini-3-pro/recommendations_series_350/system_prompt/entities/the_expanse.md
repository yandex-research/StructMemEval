# The Expanse

## User Opinions
- **General Perception**: Appreciates that it gets the science right and cares about the physics, unlike most sci-fi, while still telling a compelling human story.
- **Scientific Accuracy & Drama**: Believes that accurate science (like orbital mechanics) makes the show's drama feel earned. Specifically notes that when characters like Holden make decisions based on realistic physics constraints, it creates genuine tension.
- **The Protomolecule**: Appreciates that it isn't treated as "magic" but rather an alien technology that follows consistent rules. Believes this restraint makes the concept much scarier.
- **Political Factions**: Enjoys that the political factions feel real—portraying competing interests with valid points rather than a pure good vs evil dynamic.
- **Books**: Has read the series. Loved how it balanced big ideas with human-scale consequences.
