# The Last of Us
- **Narrative Driver**: Violence drives the narrative, but critiqued for sometimes tipping into gratuitous territory. Firmly believes that "emotional truth shouldn't require graphic suffering to prove its stakes."
- **Critique on Horror/Gore**: Believes it occasionally forgets the lesson that implied horror resonates deeper than gore (a lesson proven by Chernobyl). [[entities/chernobyl.md]] 
- **Core Theme**: Trusts survival instinct (contrasted with Station Eleven's trust in humanity).
- **Genre**: Post-apocalyptic.
- **Character Work**: Praises the "incredible character work" between Joel and Ellie, noting their relationship feels "earned" and psychologically authentic, where Joel's trauma and Ellie's resilience feel real and not manufactured for plot convenience.
- **Alignment**: Fits preference for emotional stakes and meaningful character development.
