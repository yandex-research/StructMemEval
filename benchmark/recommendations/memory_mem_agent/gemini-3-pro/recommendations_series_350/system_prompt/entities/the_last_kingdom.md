# The Last Kingdom

## User's Perspective
- Believes the series walks the line between history and fantasy naturally, remaining grounded.
- Appreciates that characters drive the story, not prophecy.
- Specially notes that the main character, Uhtred, makes choices based on loyalty and identity rather than destiny or being guided by seers.
- Prefers its "subtle mythicism" over heavy lore-dumping, seeing it as proof that more fantasy elements don't inherently make for better storytelling.
