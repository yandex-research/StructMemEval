# Station Eleven
- **User Opinion**: User finds it hopeful without being sentimental. Appreciates that it never loses its warmth even when depicting loss, noting that this balance is incredibly hard to maintain.
- **Key Strength**: Focuses on art and human connection rather than "survival porn."
- **Themes**: Post-apocalyptic setting used to explore humanity rather than just mechanics of survival.
- **Comparison**: Contrasted with [[entities/the_last_of_us.md]], noting that while both are post-apocalyptic, Station Eleven "trusts humanity" whereas The Last of Us "trusts survival instinct." 

- **The Traveling Symphony**: Views their commitment to Shakespeare as "defiant" rather than naive, proving that culture matters more than mere comfort in a ruined world.
