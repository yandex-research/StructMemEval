# Shrinking
- genre: Comedy/Drama
- theme: Grief, therapy, human connection
- cast: Harrison Ford (described as a "revelation" playing a grumpy therapist)
- tone: Combines the emotional honesty of The Bear with the warmth of Ted Lasso, but with sharper, more grounded edges. Not afraid to "sit with discomfort," balancing humor with profundity.
- character_analysis: Praised Jimmy's grief for feeling real and messy, not resolved in a tidy arc, reflecting real life.
