# Georgia O'Keeffe
- Artist type: American Modernist painter
- Known for: Paintings of enlarged flowers, New York skyscrapers, and New Mexico landscapes
- Style: Recognized for her unique and personal style, often associated with the American Southwest
- Specific Interest: User is captivated by her desert flower paintings.
- Art interpretation: User believes her work demonstrates that "feminine" subjects can be radical when viewed through an unapologetic lens.
- Art interpretation: Views her work as an act of claiming space for the overlooked and for women, embodying a form of "quiet power" rather than loud aggression.
