# Richard Serra
- artist_type: Sculptor
- style: Minimalism, Process Art
- material: Weathering steel (Cor-Ten)
- user_opinion: Negative. Finds the work intimidating and physically uncomfortable without emotional reward. Describes it as "architecture that forgot it should serve human experience," creating a sense of entrapment rather than engagement.

- critique: Acknowledges technical mastery but finds it lacks "heart" or "warmth of spirit," leaving the viewer empty.
- **Emotional Impact**: Describes the steel walls as 'soul-blocking,' contrasting them unfavorably with works that offer transcendence (like Brancusi), specifically citing them as an example of art that confines rather than expands the world.
