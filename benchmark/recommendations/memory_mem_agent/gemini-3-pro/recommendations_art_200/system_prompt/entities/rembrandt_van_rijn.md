# Rembrandt van Rijn
- **Late Self-Portraits**: Viewed as a study in aging without vanity; the wrinkles map a life fully lived.
- **Use of Light**: Described as "forgiving" rather than "flattering," highlighting humanity rather than idealizing it.
- **Humanity**: Shows the "cost of being human" and renders it beautiful, standing in stark contrast to modern, curated celebrity imagery.
- **Modern Comparison**: User specifically contrasts Rembrandt's honesty with "perfected Instagram faces," preferring the former's authenticity.
- **Technique**: "Thick impasto highlights" are seen not as showy but necessary to capture how light falls on aged skin.
