# Rothko Chapel
- details: Non-denominational chapel in Houston, Texas, founded by John and Dominique de Menil.
- artist: Mark Rothko
- access: User can visit on weekday evenings after work.
- user_impact: Transformed the user's understanding of abstract art.
- user_experience: Finds the chapel setting intensifies the experience, enforcing a slower pace unlike a rushed museum gallery.
- user_memory: Sat on a bench for forty minutes watching the colors shift as the daylight changed, finding the experience unmatched by contemporary installations.
