# Edgar Degas
- medium: Pastel (preferred by user for capturing movement/fatigue better than oils)
- specific_observation: Finds that the smudged edges of pastels capture the "breath" of movement in ways oils cannot.
- subject: Dancers
- user_reaction: Surprised by the pastel dancers
- user_appreciation: Admires how he captures exhaustion and concentration (the "labor behind perfection") rather than just staged performance.
- user_observation: Finds beauty in his depiction of "fleeting grace" when subjects think they are unobserved.
- user_interpretation: Describes the work as "stolen moments" that act as a witness to truth rather than flattery.
