# Paul Cézanne
- role: Post-Impressionist painter
- significance: Bridge between late 19th-century Impressionism and early 20th-century Cubism
- known_for: Still lifes, landscapes (Mont Sainte-Victoire), bathers

## User's Perspective
- **Still Lifes**: Views apples as "geological forms testing gravity" rather than just fruit.
- **Perspective**: Sees tilted tables as rebuilding perspective from human doubt.
- **Technique**: Appreciates how his brushstrokes "leave seams," making the world appear as if it is "still being stitched together"—a quality preferred over smooth digital perfection.
- **Philosophy**: Believes Cézanne's work proves that doubt can be a more powerful artistic force than certainty.
- **Perception vs. Photorealism**: Feels Cézanne's apples are "more real" than photorealistic renderings because they acknowledge the "struggle of perception" rather than just copying surface detail.
