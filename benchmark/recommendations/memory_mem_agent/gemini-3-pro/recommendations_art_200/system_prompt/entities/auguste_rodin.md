# Auguste Rodin
- occupation: Sculptor
- style: Known for "unfinished" rough textures that make the human form seem caught mid-breath.
- user_opinion: Finds the rough textures make stone seem like it is learning to be flesh; appreciates the lack of finish as a positive attribute. Contrasts this with "smooth marble gods," feeling Rodin's figures are more human because they show the "struggle of becoming." His work possesses a magnetic quality that compels the viewer to circle closer.
