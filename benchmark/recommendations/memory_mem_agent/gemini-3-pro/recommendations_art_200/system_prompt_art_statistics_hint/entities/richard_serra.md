# Richard Serra
- period: Minimalism, Process Art
- notable_works: Tilted Arc, The Matter of Time, Snake
- technique: Large-scale sheet metal assemblies, industrial fabrication
- materials: Weatherproof steel (Cor-Ten)
- user_sentiment: Negative
- user_feedback: Finds the work intimidating and physically uncomfortable. Describes feeling "trapped by the material" rather than transformed. Interprets the scale as aggressive rather than inviting.

- spatial_interaction: negative (feels like imprisonment/induces escape)
- architectural_critique: negative (feels like architecture that ignores human experience)
- user_feedback: Critiques his walls for 'blocking the soul' and acting as massive obstructions rather than spiritual elevation.
