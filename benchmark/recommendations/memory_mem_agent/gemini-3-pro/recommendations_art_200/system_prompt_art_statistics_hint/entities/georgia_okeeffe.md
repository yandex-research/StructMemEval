# Georgia O'Keeffe
- period: American Modernism
- notable_works: Jimson Weed, Black Iris, Ram's Head White Hollyhock and Little Hills
- technique: Large-scale close-ups, smooth gradients, finding abstraction in nature
- materials: Oil on canvas
- themes: Desert landscapes, flowers, bones, New Mexico
- specific_praise: Magnified flowers viewed as 'landscapes of intimacy' where scale shifts create sacred strangeness.
- user_sentiment: Positive (captivated by desert flowers, views magnified works as intimate landscapes)
- comparison: Praised for finding power in organic forms, unlike male contemporaries obsessed with machinery.
- specific_praise: Proves 'feminine' subjects can be radical through an unapologetic lens.

- specific_praise: "Claimed space—for women, for the overlooked, for quiet power" (values the political/social weight of her quiet subjects)
