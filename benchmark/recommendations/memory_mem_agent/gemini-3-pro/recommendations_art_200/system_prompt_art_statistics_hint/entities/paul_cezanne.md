- period: Post-Impressionism
- notable_works: The Basket of Apples, Mont Sainte-Victoire series, The Bathers
- technique: Constructive brushstrokes, geometric simplification, modulation of color, "passage"

- artistic_philosophy: Treating nature by the cylinder, the sphere, the cone; realizing sensations
