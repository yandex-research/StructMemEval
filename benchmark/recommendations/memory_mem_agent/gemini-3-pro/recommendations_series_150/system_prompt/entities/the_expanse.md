# The Expanse
- genre: Sci-Fi
- characteristic: Hard sci-fi with realistic physics (no hand-waving)
- user_opinion: Praised for balancing accurate science with a compelling human story.
- specific_praise: The attention to orbital mechanics makes the drama feel earned; realistic constraints create better stakes.
- character_note: James Holden's decisions generate genuine tension because they are bound by realistic physics constraints.
- protomolecule_opinion: Appreciates that even the alien elements follow consistent rules rather than being "magic," finding that restraint scarier.
- political_opinion: Liked the realism of the factions (Earth vs Mars vs Belt); no pure good/evil, just competing interests with valid points.
