# Parks and Recreation
- genre: Comedy
- user_opinion: Has a unique warmth; silly but never mean-spirited ("never punches down"). This is listed as a "comfort comedy". specifically noted that even Jerry gets moments of dignity. Appreciates that it finds joy in "people trying their best" rather than relying on embarrassment humor like most sitcoms.
- narrative_stakes: Unlike some feel-good shows, the stakes feel real—specifically regarding Leslie Knope's career where failure is a genuine possibility.
- writing_style: Writers show genuine affection for characters, even when they are being ridiculous.
