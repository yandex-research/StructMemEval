# Heat (1995)
- type: Movie
- genre: Action/Crime

## User Opinions
- **General**: Called it the perfect action film. Believes it still holds up after 25 years because it focused on character and practical filmmaking rather than chasing visual trends.
- **Action**: specifically praised the downtown shootout for using real blanks. Appreciates the "weight of gunfire", real bystander reactions, and the fact that De Niro and Pacino were reacting to the actual noise rather than just acting. Finds CGI shootouts fake by comparison.
- **Characters**: Praises the character work by the "two master actors" (De Niro and Pacino), noting that modern films often forget character matters as much as action.
- **Key Scenes**: Specifically highlighted the coffee shop scene as a prime example of character work.
- **Rewatch Frequency**: Rewatches every few months.
- **Atmosphere**: Notes that even the slow scenes hold tension.
