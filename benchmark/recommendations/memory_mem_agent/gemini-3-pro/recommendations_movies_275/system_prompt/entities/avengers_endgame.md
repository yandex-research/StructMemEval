# Avengers: Endgame
- user_opinion: Liked it despite general dislike of the genre.
- reason: Earned its payoff after 11 years of character work.
- contrast: Unlike most superhero films that just "throw CGI at the screen," this had narrative weight.
- key_takeaway: Proves that even CGI-heavy films work if the character foundation is built over time.

## Liked Moments
- **Tony Stark's sacrifice**: Hit hard because the audience saw him grow over time.
- **Captain America's dance**: Felt earned through years of history with Peggy Carter.
- **The Final Battle**: Worked despite heavy CGI because the audience knew the heroes and their relationships deeply.
- **Female Heroes Scene**: Specifically mentioned Captain Marvel arriving with the female heroes gave them "chills." Felt it worked because it was "earned, not just fan service."
