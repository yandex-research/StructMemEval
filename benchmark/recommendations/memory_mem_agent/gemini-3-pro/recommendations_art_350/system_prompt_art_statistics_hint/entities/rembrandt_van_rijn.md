# Rembrandt van Rijn
- movement: Baroque
- notable_works: The Night Watch, Self-Portraits (series), The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp
- technique: Chiaroscuro, Impasto, distinct capturing of light and shadow
- themes: Biblical scenes, history painting, self-portraits, aging, human condition
- user_sentiment: Positive
- user_notes: Appreciates the autobiographical nature of his self-portraits ("chronicle a life").
- user_notes: Values the "aging without vanity" in late works; finds the lighting "forgiving" rather than flattering.
- user_notes: Values authenticity over perfection ("shows the cost of being human—and makes it beautiful" unlike modern curated images).
- user_notes: Admires impasto technique not as showy but necessary ("capture how light actually falls on aged skin").
