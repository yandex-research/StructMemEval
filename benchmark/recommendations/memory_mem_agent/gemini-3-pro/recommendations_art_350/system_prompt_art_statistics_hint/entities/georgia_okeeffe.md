# Georgia O'Keeffe
- period: American Modernism
- notable_subjects: Desert flowers, New Mexico landscapes, animal skulls
- technique: Large scale, bold colors, synthesis of abstraction and representation
- user_sentiment: Positive
- specific_interest: Desert flowers
- user_insight: Sees magnified flowers as "landscapes of intimacy" where scale shift makes the familiar sacred
- user_insight: Values her organic power as distinct from machinery-obsessed male contemporaries; appreciates the lack of romanticization.
- user_insight: Sees her treatment of 'feminine' subjects as radical because it's unapologetic, not delicate.
- user_insight: Appreciates how she "claimed space" for women and the overlooked, valuing the "quiet power" in her work.
