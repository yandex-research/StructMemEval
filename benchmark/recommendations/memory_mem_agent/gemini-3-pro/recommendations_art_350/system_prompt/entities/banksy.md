# Banksy
- **Artist Type**: Street artist, political activist, film director.
- **Style**: Stencil graffiti, satirical, dark humor, subversive epigrams.
- **Themes**: Anti-war, anti-capitalism, anti-establishment.

## User Perspective
- **Critique**: Acknowledges the commentary is sharp but believes the work loses its soul when removed from the street and framed in galleries.
- **Context Preference**: Argues the art is designed to "ambush" the viewer in public spaces ("grimy walls"), contrasting this authentic encounter with the artificiality of "cocktail hour" gallery viewing.
- **Critical Distinction**: Respects the political message/content more than the aesthetic execution.
