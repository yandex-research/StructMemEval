# Sandro Botticelli
- era: Renaissance
- notable_works: The Birth of Venus, Primavera
- **User Interpretation**: User finds his emotional restraint more potent than Baroque drama because it invites the viewer to "lean in" rather than being overwhelmed.
- **Style Contrast**: Contrasts with Baroque theatricality, focusing on subtlety and internal emotion.
- **Aesthetic Analysis**: Describes the "linear grace" of his figures as creating a rhythm that guides the eye "like poetry on a page." 
- **Sanctuary Role**: User returns to Botticelli specifically when modern art feels "too loud or aggressive," finding a necessary quietude in his work.
