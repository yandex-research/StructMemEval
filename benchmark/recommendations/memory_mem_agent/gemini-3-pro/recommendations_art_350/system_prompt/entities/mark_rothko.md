# Mark Rothko
- style: Abstract Expressionism (Color Field)
- **User Perception**: Views his color fields not as mere rectangles but as "emotional weather systems."
- **User Experience**: Describes standing before his work as a form of meditation where the painting "breathes with you."
- **Aesthetic Contrast**: Contrasts Rothko's "soft edges" with the "assault" of digital screens, noting that the paintings "invite quiet contemplation" where "you meet the work halfway."
