# Georgia O'Keeffe
- known_for: Modernism, flower paintings, desert landscapes
- subject_interest: Desert flowers
- **User Preference**: Finds her desert flowers captivating. [[user.md]]
- **User Insight**: Views her magnified flowers as "landscapes of intimacy" where scale shifts make the familiar "strange and sacred."
- **Comparative Insight**: Contrasts her approach with male contemporaries focused on machinery; appreciates how she found "power in organic forms" without succumbing to romanticism.
- **Critical Insight**: Believes her work proves that 'feminine' subjects can be radical when viewed through an unapologetic lens.
- **Feminist Interpretation**: Sees O'Keeffe not just as a painter of flowers, but as someone who "claimed space—for women, for the overlooked, for quiet power."
