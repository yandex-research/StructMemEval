# Jean-Michel Basquiat
- role: Artist
- movement: Neo-expressionism
- style: Raw, chaotic, expressive, often incorporating text and diagrams.
- association: Collaborated with **Andy Warhol**. [[entities/andy_warhol.md]]
- origin: Started as a graffiti artist under the tag SAMO.

## User Opinion
- **Aesthetic Judgement**: Finds his "raw energy" electrifying, appreciating works that balance chaos with emotional anchors.
- **Critique**: Feels alienated by the "inside jokes" of his symbols (crowns, scribbles), describing them as exclusive rather than communicative.
- **Narrative Preference**: Specifically appreciates when Basquiat's symbols serve a clear story, such as depictions of jazz musicians or historical trauma.
- **Disconnection Point**: Disconnects from the work when it appears to be "marks for marks' sake" without underlying narrative function.
