# Damien Hirst
- **Known for**: "The Physical Impossibility of Death in the Mind of Someone Living" (tiger shark in formaldehyde).
- **User Opinion**: Describes the shark piece as "iconic" but ultimately hollow.
- **Critical Metaphor**: Compares the shark to a "dorm room philosophy debate made physical," lacking genuine depth.
- **Aesthetic Judgment**: dismisses the work as "shock without substance," arguing it generates memorable headlines but remains "forgettable art."
- **Critical Comparison**: Finds Hirst's work intellectualized at the expense of emotion; argues that unlike Monet, Hirst "requires footnotes" to be meaningful. [[entities/claude_monet.md]]
