# Henri Matisse
- medium: Cut-outs (later career)
- insight: Later cut-outs changed the user's view of aging artists.
- **Artistic Philosophy**: Views the cut-outs as proof that "limitation breeds creativity"; confined to scissors and paper in old age, he produced his most joyful, daring work.
- **Key Concept**: "Constraint as liberation"—finding freedom within boundaries.
- **Comparative Insight**: Contrasts Matisse's constant reinvention with artists who "repeat past successes," noting his cut-outs have "more vitality than most young artists' work today." 
- **Aesthetic Description**: Describes the cut-outs as "pure joy made visible," characterized by a lack of pretension and the interplay of "color and form dancing together." 
- **Healing Potential**: The user believes Matisse's cut-outs possess an inherent joy that could "heal more than medicine," specifically envisioning them in a children's hospital.
