# Edgar Degas
- role: Artist
- movement: Impressionism
- known_for: Pastel drawings, dancers, depictions of movement
- user_insight: Captures dancers in unguarded moments (exhaustion, concentration), finding beauty in the "labor behind perfection" rather than just the performance. [[user.md]]
- aesthetic_judgment: Describes works as "stolen moments"—art that acts as a witness to reality rather than flattery of the subject.
- medium_analysis: Finds pastels superior to oils for capturing movement/fatigue, likening the "smudged edges" to breath.
- technique_philosophy: Views the use of pastel as "inevitable"—the only medium capable of capturing the specific moments Degas depicted.
