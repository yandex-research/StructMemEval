# Paul Cézanne
- **Current Activity**: User is studying his still lifes this week.
- **Artistic Style**: Post-Impressionism. Known for building form with color and distinct brushstrokes.
- **User Insight**: Describes his apples as "geological forms testing gravity" rather than just fruit.
- **Perspective Philosophy**: Interprets his tilted tables not as breaking perspective, but as rebuilding it from "human doubt." 
- **Realism vs Photorealism**: User believes his apples feel "more real" than photorealistic renderings because they capture the "struggle of perception" involved in seeing.
- **Philosophical Proof**: User cites Cézanne as the definitive proof that artistic **doubt** can be more powerful than **certainty**.
