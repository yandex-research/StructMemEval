# Stephen King
- profession: Author
- key_trait: Realism
- writing_style: Grounds characters in mundane reality (jobs, marriages, petty worries) before introducing horror elements. Often focuses on "ordinary characters facing extraordinary circumstances."
- user_opinion: Believes his characters feel real because of their detailed ordinary lives; they feel like they could be neighbors.
- impact: The horror feels more impactful because the people feel real first
