- Listening to music
- Audio Preference: Prefers original recordings' character over modern remasters
- Audio Format: Loves the warmth/depth of 70s analog records; feels tape saturation adds character digital lacks
- Music Discovery: Enjoys finding underground alternative bands that push boundaries
- Music Taste: Appreciates alternative rock with complex emotions and non-formulaic diversity
- Musical Interest: Fascinated by odd time signatures and music theory
- Genre Preference: Generally dislikes metal aggression (finds screaming overwhelming), but loves **Instrumental Metal** (e.g., Animals as Leaders) for its technical proficiency and lack of vocals

- Vocal Preference: Specifically dislikes harsh vocals/screaming/growling; finds them distracting and mistakenly makes songs sound identical to one another
- Violin: Currently working on Bach's Partita No. 3
- Violin Insight: Realized the high degree of coordination required between bow and fingers
- Classical Music Appreciation: Learning violin unlocked appreciation for technical skill; now enjoys pieces previously found boring. However, finds long instrumental pieces difficult to follow without lyrics to mark sections.
- Live Classical Perspective: Seeing a live orchestra changed their perspective. The physical presence of sound and visual musician interaction made the music come alive in a way recordings never could.
- Production Preference: Loves lo-fi production and classic rock recording techniques (live in studio, minimal overdubs). Values raw, unpolished sound where human imperfections are audible, believing this gives the music soul. Finds this more honest/authentic than modern, over-produced polish.
- Orchestral Insight: Fascinated by the communication between conductor and musicians during performance
- Classical Guitar Insight: Playing classical guitar deepened appreciation for orchestral music by revealing guitar-like textures in string arrangements and clarifying bridges between solo and ensemble compositions.
- Auto-Tune Sensitivity: Claims to always distinguish when auto-tune is overused.
