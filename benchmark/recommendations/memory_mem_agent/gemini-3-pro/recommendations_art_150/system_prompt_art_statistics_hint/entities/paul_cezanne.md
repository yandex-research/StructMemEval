# Paul Cézanne
- period: Post-Impressionism
- notable_genres: Still Lifes, Landscapes (Mont Sainte-Victoire)
- technique: Constructive brushstrokes, modulation of color, geometric simplification
- user_sentiment: Positive (values structural depth, visible construction/seams, and reconstructed perspective over digital perfection)
- user_quotes: "apples aren't just fruit—they're geological forms testing gravity", "tilted tables...rebuild perspective from human doubt", "brushstrokes leave seams—like the world is still being stitched together", "doubt can be more powerful than certainty", "apples feel more real than photorealistic renderings because they acknowledge the struggle of perception"
- relations: Bridge between Impressionism and Cubism
