# Edgar Degas
- **Medium**: Pastel
- **Subject**: Dancers
- **User Reaction**: Surprised by his pastel dancers.
- **Key Insight**: Captures dancers in unguarded moments—exhaustion, concentration, fleeting grace. Finds beauty in the labor behind perfection.
- **Aesthetic**: Appreciates the "stolen moments" — art acting as a witness rather than flattery.

- **Technique**: Notes that the pastel medium "smudged edges" capture movement and fatigue like "breath," unlike oils.
