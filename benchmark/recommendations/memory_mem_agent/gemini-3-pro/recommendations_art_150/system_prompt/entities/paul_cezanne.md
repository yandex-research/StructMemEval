# Paul Cézanne
- role: Artist / Painter
- associated_style: Post-Impressionism
- user_interest: Studying his still lifes.
- user_observation: Describes his apples as "geological forms testing gravity" and his tilted tables as rebuilding perspective from human doubt.
- user_observation: Contrasts his work with digital renders; his visible brushstrokes leave "seams," implying the world is still being stitched together.
- user_observation: Believes his work proves that doubt can be more powerful than certainty in art.
- user_observation: Finds his apples more real than photorealistic renderings because they acknowledge the "struggle of perception" rather than just presenting a finished image.
