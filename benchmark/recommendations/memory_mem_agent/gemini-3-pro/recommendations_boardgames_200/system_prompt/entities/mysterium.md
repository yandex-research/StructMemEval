# Mysterium (Board Game)
- premise: Cooperative deduction where a Ghost gives visual clues to Mediums to solve a murder.
- mechanics: Asymmetric cooperative gameplay; restricted communication (visual only).
- **User Feedback (Negative)**: The game's structure **failed to prevent quarterbacking**. Contrary to hopes that hidden info would help, **one dominant player (acting as a medium)** took over decision-making by **interpreting all visions for the group**. 
    - The user noted that while the "medium" analyzed everything, the other players (referred to as "ghosts" in this context) **became passive**.
    - **Specific Complaint**: "The ghosts just waited for instructions most of the game."
    - **Structural Critique**: "The medium role had all the interpretive power while ghosts had execution-only tasks." This reinforces the feeling of **total lack of agency**, similar to their experience with *Pandemic* and *Robinson Crusoe*.
