# Avengers: Endgame
- **Genre**: Superhero
- **User Opinion**: Positive exception to general superhero fatigue.
- **Why it Worked**: It earned its payoff after 11 years of character work.
- **Comparison**: Contrasted with most superhero films that just "throw CGI at the screen" without the narrative foundation.
- **Key Element**: The emotional weight came from the long-term character investment, not the visual spectacle.
- **Specific Moments**:
    - **Tony Stark's Sacrifice**: "Hit hard" because the audience saw him grow throughout the franchise.
    - **Captain America's Ending**: The dance with Peggy Carter felt "earned through years of history" rather than being a cheap moment.
