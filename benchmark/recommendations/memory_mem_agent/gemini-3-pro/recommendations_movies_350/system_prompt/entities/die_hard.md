# Die Hard
- **User Opinion**: Considers it the "perfect Christmas movie" and the "perfect example" of an ideal film.
- **Key Attributes**: Tight, efficient, satisfying. "No wasted scenes."
- **Pacing**: Perfect pacing (2h 12m).
- **Action**: Unmatched. Appreciates that McClane feels human and vulnerable ("believe his pain") unlike modern invincible CGI heroes.
- **Villain**: Hans Gruber. Features the "perfect villain payoff" when he falls from the roof.
- **Cast**:
    - **Bruce Willis**: John McClane.
    - **Alan Rickman**: Hans Gruber. "Incredible" in the role.
- **Writing**: Praised for giving every character clear motives.
- **Comparison**: Cited as superior to modern superhero films due to practical filmmaking and real stakes.
- **Sequels**: Feels they never matched the original's quality.
