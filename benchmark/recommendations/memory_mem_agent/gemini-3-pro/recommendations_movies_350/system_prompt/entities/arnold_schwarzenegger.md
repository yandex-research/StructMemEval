# Arnold Schwarzenegger
- **Occupation**: Actor, former bodybuilder, former politician.
- **User Opinion**: Considers him a key figure in "real action" movies. Specifically praises his "peak" era in the 80s/90s.
- **Key Roles**:
    - **Terminator 2**: Considered his "absolute peak". His presence and one-liners are what make the movie. The user prefers his physical performance over modern CGI.
    - **The Terminator**: Liked him as the villain.
    - **Predator**: Part of the "perfect cast" that felt like real soldiers. Specifically praised the mud camouflage scene as "real action" utilizing environment over artificial solutions.
    - **Commando**: Cited as "peak Schwarzenegger" and "pure 80s action perfection". Specifically noted the mall shootout and the line "I'll be back".
- **Attributes**: Values his physical presence and charisma, contrasting it with "weightless" modern CGI heroes. Noted that he was "physically imposing and funny" in his prime, understanding that action heroes need personality.
- **Stunt Work**: The user views him as the standard for action stars who didn't rely on doubles, unlike "modern stars".
