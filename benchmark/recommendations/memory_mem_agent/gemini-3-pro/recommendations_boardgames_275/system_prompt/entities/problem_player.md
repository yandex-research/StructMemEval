# The Problem Player
- **Role**: A regular member of the gaming group.
- **Behavior**: consistently "quarterbacks" (alpha games) in cooperative settings.
- **Behavior**: Directs other players' turns even after explicit group discussions asking them to stop.
- **Behavior**: Even attempts to direct play in hidden information games like *The Crew*.
- **Impact**: This behavior is the primary driver for the user's dislike of standard cooperative games.
- **Gaming Strategy**: The group has decided to stick to competitive games when he is around to avoid the quarterbacking issue.
