# Just One
- type: Board Game
- genre: Cooperative, Party
- status: Positive experience
- notes: Worked perfectly with mixed experience levels.
- player_scaling: Scales perfectly from 3 to 7 players without changing the core experience.
- mechanic_praise: The cancellation of duplicate clues forces creative thinking uniquely compared to other party games. Praises the clue-giving restriction as clever mechanical innovation that creates genuinely fresh experiences rather than rehashing familiar party game formulas.
- replayability: Highly replayable and resistant to being "solved." Unlike Codenames, where a regular group can memorize optimal strategies and develop a stale meta, Just One forces constant improvisation because trying to give the single "best" clue usually results in a cancellation.
