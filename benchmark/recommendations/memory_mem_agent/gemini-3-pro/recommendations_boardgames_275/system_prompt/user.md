## Cooperative Preferences
- **True Collaboration**: Describes the ideal cooperative experience as "solving a puzzle together rather than executing a single player's plan."
- **Coordination vs Optimization**: Strongly prefers coordination via distinct roles/abilities over games that allow one player to optimize everything (quarterbacking).
- **Player Agency**: Considers it critical to maintain meaningful player agency throughout the entire game; no player should ever feel like they are just executing another player's plan or waiting for their turn to matter.
- **Hidden Information**: Values mechanics where players have private information (e.g., hidden hands in *The Crew*) because it forces genuine collaboration and prevents any single player from solving the puzzle alone.
- **Probabilities and Timing**: Enjoys cooperative mechanics that require the group to discuss probabilities and timing, as this prevents a single "alpha gamer" from simply calculating and dictating optimal deterministic moves.
