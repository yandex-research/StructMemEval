# Henri Matisse
- **Late Career**: Known for his "cut-outs" created during his later years when health issues limited his ability to paint.
- **User Connection**: Views the cut-outs as proof that "limitation breeds creativity"; specifically admires how being "confined to scissors and paper" resulted in his most "joyful, daring work," seeing this constraint as a form of liberation.
- **Reinvention**: Admires Matisse for constantly reinventing himself rather than repeating past successes.
- **Vitality**: Believes his late cut-outs possess "more vitality than most young artists' work today." 
- **Style**: Fauvism, Modernism.

- **Aesthetic Description**: Describes the cut-outs as "pure joy made visible," noting a lack of pretension and a sense of "color and form dancing together."
- **Healing Power**: Believes his cut-outs possess a "healing" quality strong enough to replace medicine in a children's hospital setting, viewing the joy they radiate as physically curative.
