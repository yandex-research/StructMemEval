# Paul Cézanne
- **Nationality**: French
- **Role**: Post-Impressionist painter
- **Known For**: Bridging late 19th-century Impressionism and early 20th-century Cubism.

## User Interpretation
- **Apples as Geology**: Views his apples not as fruit but as "geological forms testing gravity," emphasizing their weight and permanence.
- **Perspective of Doubt**: Interprets his tilted tables not as errors but as a deliberate rebuilding of perspective from "human doubt," suggesting a psychological dimension to his spatial distortions.
- **Struggle of Perception**: Believes his apples feel "more real than photorealistic renderings" specifically because they acknowledge the "struggle of perception" rather than just mimicking optics.
