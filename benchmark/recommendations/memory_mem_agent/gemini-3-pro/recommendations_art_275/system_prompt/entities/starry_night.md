# The Starry Night
- **Artist**: [[entities/vincent_van_gogh.md]]
- **Year**: 1889
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Current Location**: Museum of Modern Art (MoMA), New York City
- **Description**: Depicts the view from the east-facing window of Van Gogh's asylum room at Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, just before sunrise, with the addition of an idealized village.

## User Perception
- **Emotional Weather**: The user interprets the painting not just as scenery but as "emotional weather," seeing the swirling skies as a representation of "hope fighting through mental storms," transforming inner turbulence into enduring beauty.
- **Texture vs. Digital**: The user criticizes digital reproductions for flattening the work into "wallpaper," while the real texture reveals the artist's "hand trembling with purpose," emphasizing the physical struggle in the paint application.
- **Reproduction vs. Reality Quote**: "In reproduction, it's a pretty night sky. In person, it's a document of human struggle transformed into beauty."
