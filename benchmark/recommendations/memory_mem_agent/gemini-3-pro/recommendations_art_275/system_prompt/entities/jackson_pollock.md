# Jackson Pollock
- **Occupation**: American painter, major figure in abstract expressionism.
- **Current Relevance**: There is currently a Jackson Pollock exhibit downtown.
- **User Critique**: Finds his energy undeniable but notes that without biographical context, the work can feel random.
- **Compositional Intelligence**: Believes his best works reveal a hidden structure or "compositional intelligence" beneath the chaos.
- **Number 1A Critique**: Specifically praised *Number 1A* as feeling "deliberate," contrasting it with later works.
- **Later Works Critique**: Views later pieces as "diminishing returns" where the technique overshadows meaning, aligning with a general dislike of "flash" over substance.
