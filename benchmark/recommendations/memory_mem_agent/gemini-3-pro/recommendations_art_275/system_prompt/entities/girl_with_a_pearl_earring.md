# Girl with a Pearl Earring
- **Artist**: [[entities/johannes_vermeer.md]]
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Period**: Dutch Golden Age
- **Description**: Tronie painting (study of a head) often referred to as the "Mona Lisa of the North."

## User Insights
- **Quiet Conversation**: Described as holding "centuries of quiet conversation" in her gaze.
- **Narrative Depth**: The "tear of light" on the earring is seen as containing more storytelling than most novels.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Values the subtle, unspoken communication over explicit drama.
