# Edgar Degas
- **Medium**: Known for pastels.
- **Subjects**: Famous for dancers.
- **Interpretation**: Captures the "labor behind perfection"—exhaustion, concentration, and fleeting grace—rather than just the performance.
- **User's View**: Describes his work as "stolen moments" and "art as witness rather than flattery," appreciating the lack of artificial polish.
- **Pastel Technique**: Values how his "smudged edges feel like breath," capturing movement and fatigue in a way oils cannot.
- **Mastery of Medium**: Believes Degas makes his technique feel "inevitable," as if pastel was the absolute only way to capture the specific moment.
