# Sandro Botticelli
- **Artistic Restraint**: Contrasts his style with later Baroque drama; finds the restraint makes emotion more potent.
- **Intimacy**: Values the "lean in" quality of his work rather than being "overwhelmed" by dramatic excess.
- **Refuge**: Returns to Botticelli as a sanctuary when modern art feels too "loud or aggressive." 

- **Linear Rhythm**: Describes the "linear grace of his figures" as creating a rhythm that "guides your eye like poetry on a page."
