# Georgia O'Keeffe
- **Desert Flowers**: The user is captivated by her depictions of desert flowers.
- **Style**: Known for magnifying the natural world, particularly flowers and desert landscapes.
- **Intimacy of Scale**: User perceives her magnified flowers as "landscapes of intimacy" rather than mere botanical studies.
- **Sacred Familiarity**: The shift in scale is noted to make familiar subjects feel "strange and sacred" simultaneously.
- **Contrast with Contemporaries**: User notes that unlike her male contemporaries who were obsessed with machinery, O'Keeffe found power in organic forms without romanticizing them.
- **Radical Femininity**: User believes O'Keeffe's work proves that "feminine" subjects (like flowers) become radical when viewed through an "unapologetic lens," rejecting dismissal.

- **Claiming Space**: Views her work as more than painting flowers; it is an act of claiming space for women, the overlooked, and for quiet power.
