# Frida Kahlo
- **Occupation**: Mexican painter known for her many portraits, self-portraits, and works inspired by the nature and artifacts of Mexico.
- **Style**: Employed a naïve folk art style to explore questions of identity, postcolonialism, gender, class, and race in Mexican society.
- **Key Themes**: Physical pain, emotional suffering, identity, Mexican culture, and the transformation of trauma into power.

## User Interpretations
- **The Stare**: Observes that Kahlo's physical pain is transmuted into spiritual power specifically through her "unbroken eye contact," which forces the viewer to engage rather than look away.
- **Post-Exhibit**: Left the exhibit feeling "shattered and strengthened," validating this emotional duality as the mark of "real art."
