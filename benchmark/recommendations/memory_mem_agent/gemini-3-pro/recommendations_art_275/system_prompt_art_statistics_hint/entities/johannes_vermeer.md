# Johannes Vermeer
- period: Baroque
- notable_works: Girl with a Pearl Earring, The Milkmaid, View of Delft
- technique: Mastery of light (chiaroscuro), expensive pigments (ultramarine), optical precision
- materials: Oil on canvas
- user_sentiment: Positive
- themes: Domestic interior scenes, quietude, mystery
- user_feedback: Values the subtlety and layering; rewards patience unlike 'flashy contemporary' pieces.
- user_observation: Describes his work as a 'sanctuary for slow looking' in contrast to the 'fast-scrolling world'.
