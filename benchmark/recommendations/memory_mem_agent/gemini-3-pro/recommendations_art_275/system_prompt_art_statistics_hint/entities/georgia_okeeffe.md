# Georgia O'Keeffe
- period: American Modernism
- notable_works: Jimson Weed, Cow's Skull: Red, White, and Blue, Black Iris
- technique: Magnified scale, smooth gradients, abstraction of nature
- materials: Oil on canvas
- user_sentiment: Positive ("captivate me")
- user_insight: "landscapes of intimacy"; values the scale shift that makes the familiar "strange and sacred"

- themes: Desert landscapes, flowers, bones, New Mexico
- distinction: "found power in organic forms without romanticizing them" unlike male contemporaries obsessed with machinery
- user_insight_philosophy: saw her work as "claiming space—for women, for the overlooked, for quiet power"
