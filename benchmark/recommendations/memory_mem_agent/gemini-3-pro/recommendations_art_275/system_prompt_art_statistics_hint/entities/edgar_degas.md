# Edgar Degas
- period: Impressionism
- notable_works: The Dance Class, The Little Fourteen-Year-Old Dancer, L'Absinthe
- technique: Pastel manipulation, obsession with movement, off-center composition
- materials: Pastel, Oil, Bronze
- user_sentiment: Positive (Surprised)
- themes: Ballet dancers, racehorses, bathing women

- user_insight: values the depiction of exhaustion and concentration over the polished performance
- quote: "Finds beauty in the labor behind perfection"
- user_insight_2: values 'stolen moments' and 'art as witness' (anti-flattery)
- quote_2: "Unlike polished portraits, these feel like stolen moments"

- user_insight_3: prefers pastel over oil for capturing 'movement and fatigue'
- quote_3: "smudged edges feel like breath"
- user_insight_4: views technique as 'inevitable' to the subject, not just a choice
- quote_4: "makes technique feel inevitable—like the only way to capture that moment was through pastel"
