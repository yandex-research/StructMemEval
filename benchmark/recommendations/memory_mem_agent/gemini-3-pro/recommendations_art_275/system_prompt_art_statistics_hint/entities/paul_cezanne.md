# Paul Cézanne
- period: Post-Impressionism
- notable_works: The Basket of Apples, Still Life with Plaster Cupid, Mont Sainte-Victoire
- technique: Constructive stroke, geometric simplification, shifting perspectives
- themes: Still lifes, landscapes, bathers
- significance: Bridge between Impressionism and Cubism; "father of us all" (Picasso/Matisse)

- user_sentiment: Positive (values the visible 'doubt' and process over polished certainty)

- user_insight: Finds his apples 'more real' than photorealism because they acknowledge the struggle of perception.
