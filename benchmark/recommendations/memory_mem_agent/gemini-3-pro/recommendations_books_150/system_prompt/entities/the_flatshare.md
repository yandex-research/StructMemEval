# The Flatshare
- **Author**: Beth O'Leary
- **Genre**: Romance / Contemporary Fiction
- **User Opinion**: Positive. Praised for having obstacles derived from real-life circumstances rather than manufactured misunderstandings. Fits user's preference for avoiding the miscommunication trope. User specifically noted caring about Tiffy's career success almost as much as the romantic outcome.
## Characters
- **Tiffy**: Character with career ambitions that predated and continued alongside her relationship.
- **Leon**: Character with family obligations that predated and continued alongside his relationship.
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Notable for how individual lives (career/family) co-existed with the romance rather than being subsumed by it. User noted that the relationship enhanced their individual journeys instead of replacing them.
