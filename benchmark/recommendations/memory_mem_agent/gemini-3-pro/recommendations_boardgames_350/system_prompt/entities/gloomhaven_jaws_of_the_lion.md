# Gloomhaven: Jaws of the Lion
- **Relation**: Standalone prequel/expansion to [[entities/gloomhaven.md]].
- **Role**: Often serves as a "gateway" product due to its improved tutorialization.
- **Key Differences**:
    - **Map Book**: Replaces map tiles, significantly reducing setup/teardown time.
    - **Tutorial**: First 5 scenarios teach rules progressively (Learning by Doing).
    - **Length**: Shorter campaign (approx. 25 scenarios).
