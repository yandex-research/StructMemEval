# Scythe
- **Type**: Board Game (Engine Building, Area Control)
- **Combat System**: Deterministic (no dice). Players use a Power dial (spending 'Power' resource) and add Combat Cards to determine the total strength.
- **Complexity**: Medium weight. Less complex than Twilight Imperium.
- **Style**: "Cold War" feel - combat is expensive and often infrequent; the threat of combat is a major strategic element.
