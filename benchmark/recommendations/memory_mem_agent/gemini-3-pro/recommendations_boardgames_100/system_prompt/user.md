# User Information

## Hobbies
- **Board Games**: Loves strategy games requiring deep thinking and long-term planning. While generally preferring strategic depth, has explicitly realized they shouldn't judge party games by the same standards as strategy games; values party games primarily for their ability to bring people together rather than their mechanics.
  - **Storage**: Currently finds that small card games are cluttering shelf space, indicating a need for better organization or consolidation.
  - **Preferences**: Dislikes cooperative games prone to the 'alpha player' phenomenon; finds games with **hidden information or limited communication** work better as they naturally prevent dominating decision-making.
  - **Complexity Preference**: Seeks strategic depth but specifically avoids 'unnecessary complexity', massive rulebooks, and games that take hours to learn. Explicitly notes that easier setup leads to more frequent plays; specifically, setup time kills momentum for quick weeknight games.
  - **Abstract Strategy**: Loves games like Azul and Santorini for balancing simple rules with deep strategy. Deeply values **physical aesthetics and visual design**, finding beautiful components significantly enhance the satisfaction of gameplay. Dislikes abstract games that become predictable or "solved" after a few plays once optimal strategies are learned.
  - **Mechanics Preference**: Now leaning towards **randomness or hidden information** to keep games fresh. Strictly prefers **high player interaction** where decisions actively impact opponents, avoiding "multiplayer solitaire".
  - Terraforming Mars: Played 3 times (last played recently). Finds seeing the engine develop over rounds incredibly satisfying, though recently feels it is becoming repetitive as everyone learns the best strategies. Notes it sometimes feels like 'multiplayer solitaire' due to limited player interaction. **Status**: Planning an upcoming marathon session.
  - Codenames: Played at game night last weekend. Acknowledges it lacks depth but enjoyed it for getting the family talking and laughing.
  - Pandemic Legacy: Played recently with a group. Disliked the experience because one person made all the decisions ('quarterbacking'), making it feel like playing solo with spectators.
  - Spirit Island: Described as the perfect cooperative experience because it avoids alpha player issues and offers an amazing feeling of shared victory. [[entities/spirit_island.md]]
  - The Crew: Worked really well for the group. Considers it a refreshing cooperative experience because hidden hands forced everyone to contribute, preventing alpha gaming.
  - Azul: Played every evening this week. Finds the tile placement deeply satisfying, specifically citing the **weight and colorful design** of the tiles as key factors. Appreciates that each game feels different.
  - Twilight Imperium: Looked into it but was intimidated by the rulebook.
  - Wavelength: A rare exception to their dislike of party games. Enjoys it because it creates genuine moments of connection and surprise.
  - Hive: Got tired of it after about 10 games. Found the opening moves too formulaic and outcomes predictable early on.
- **Scythe**: Interested in it as a more approachable alternative. Asking about combat complexity. [[entities/scythe.md]]
- **Root**: Considers it "perfect" due to total asymmetry where every faction plays completely differently. [[entities/root.md]]
