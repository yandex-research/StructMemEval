# Rembrandt van Rijn
- Art Observation: Self-portraits viewed as a chronicle of a life.
- Interpretation: Late self-portraits show aging without vanity; wrinkles map a life fully lived.
- Aesthetic: His light doesn't flatter; it forgives (values the honest depiction of humanity).
- Critique: Contrasts Rembrandt's honest depiction of the "cost of being human" (beautiful in its reality) with modern curated celebrity images.
